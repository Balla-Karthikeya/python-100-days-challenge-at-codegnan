Data Models

 • Student Model – ID, Name, Department, Phone, Email

 • Faculty Model – ID, Name, Department, Email, Phone, Subjects, Designation

 • Department Model – Department Name, HOD Name, Email

 • University Model – University Name, Principal Name, Location



Service & Operations

 • Department Service – View department faculty, add subjects, view faculty details

 • University Service – Add/Remove students and faculty, manage departments

 • Subject Service – Add subjects, link subjects to departments, and assign faculty

 • Analytics Service – View total students, staff, and faculty
